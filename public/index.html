Multi-Objective Fleet Optimization Formulation

The platform formulates maritime logistics as a discrete combinatorial optimization problem over route allocation bitstrings, minimizing composite penalized operational fitness:

F(x) = w_f * (f_fuel(x) / N_fuel) + w_c * (f_cost(x) / N_cost) + w_e * (f_emiss(x) / N_emiss) + Penalty(x)

Naval architecture hydrodynamics utilize the cubic law of propulsion power combined with Admiralty displacement coefficient scaling (2/3 power of total vessel plus cargo displacement).

Platform Disclosures & Architecture
  • Synthetic Data: Illustrative prototype parameters modeled from AIS traffic and IMO GHG Study benchmarks. Not for live voyage navigation.
  • Quantum-Inspired Algorithm: Classical CPU heuristic utilizing Q-bit rotation gates and H-gate catastrophic disruption recovery. Runs entirely on classical hardware.
  • Serverless Architecture: Powered by FastAPI and Vercel Serverless Functions with instant JSON serialization.
