Multi-Objective Fleet Optimization Formulation
Mathematical formulation of combinatorial fleet assignment and speed scheduling

The platform formulates maritime fleet deployment as a high-dimensional combinatorial discrete optimization problem. The objective minimizes a normalized composite scalar fitness combining three competing operational priorities:

F(x) = w_fuel · [f_fuel(x) / N_fuel] + w_cost · [f_cost(x) / N_cost] + w_emiss · [f_emiss(x) / N_emiss] + Π(x)

Where x ∈ {0, 1}ᴸ is the binary decision bitstring encoding vessel assignments, route speed selections, and shore power toggles; w_fuel + w_cost + w_emiss = 1.0 are normalized priority weights; and Π(x) = Σ λ_k · max(0, g_k(x)) is an exterior penalty function enforcing demand satisfaction, fleet availability limits, service frequency, and schedule reliability constraints.
